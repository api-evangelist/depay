# Depay

> Depay (Depayments S.A.) is a Latin American cross-border payment infrastructure company that connects banks, wallets, and fintechs to the region's instant payment rails through a single API integration, enabling real-time international payments with settlement in local currency. Depay bridges interoperability across PIX (Brazil), Transferencias 3.0 (Argentina), Bre-B (Colombia), QR BCR (Peru), and QR BCB (Bolivia). Founded in Buenos Aires and backed by Techstars (Payments Powered by Stellar and MoneyGram).
